01 / Experience

Platform services across
the device lifecycle.

I lead the design, development, optimization and delivery of energy IoT backend services and business platforms, spanning device protocols, storage management, remote control, firmware updates, APIs and spare parts purchasing. I collaborate with product, testing, algorithm and customer support teams on integration, delivery and production troubleshooting.

01

Device integration & data acquisition

Led the design and implementation of device protocol parsing and a reusable point-table framework for multiple device and message types, including version compatibility, dynamic point tables, and real-time and second-level data acquisition.

02

Energy storage & battery data

Led protocol parsing and data modeling for storage devices, battery racks, packs and cells, including hierarchical mapping, array aggregation, state and alarm integration, and consistent power and energy data handling.

03

Energy management

Led software integration for commercial storage, micro-storage and home energy management, covering time-of-use schedules, demand control, self-consumption, backup, export limits, grid power limits, PV energy management, capacity expansion and dynamic tariffs.

04

Remote device operations

Led remote control service design and development for operating modes, battery charging and discharging, EV chargers, parameter reads and writes, export-limit schedules and automatic device time synchronization, with traceable tasks, callbacks, aggregation, retries, rate limiting and concurrency optimization.

05

Developer platform

Led the design, development and launch of a developer platform with remote device control, parameter reads, task queries and callbacks, including third-party integration and API compatibility.

06

Device updates & automation

Led firmware service and workflow improvements covering version reads, automated tagging, batch upgrades, retries, status queries and upgrade history, improving automation and traceability.

07

Data services & platform reliability

Led device data forwarding and reliability improvements using message queues, caching, validation, exception handling and observability to reduce latency, with cross-region integration and production incident resolution.

08

Online purchasing platform

Led the design, development and launch of a spare parts purchasing platform covering product management, guest checkout, carts, orders, Stripe payments, ERP integration, email notifications and PDF invoices, with retries, rate limiting and error alerts.
